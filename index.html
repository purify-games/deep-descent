Crypt

Stages 0-0 to 0-4

You start with the Pistol and five hearts. Every floor is a tree of rooms:

  1. Clear the room. The doors lock until every enemy is down.
  2. Pick up the coins. They are your score, and your money.
  3. Shop and open chests. Upgrades stack into a build; curses pay well and cost you.
  4. Take the portal down.

Two hands

Six weapons, carry two

  • Pistol

    Steady, mid range. The one you start with.

  • Sniper Rifle

    Hits harder the farther away. Bounces off a wall.

  • Shotgun

    Three pellets. Brutal up close, useless far off.

  • Swing Blade

    Spins round you and knocks enemy shots back.

  • Chainsaw

    Hold fire and it bites. Let it rest or it slows.

  • Railgun

    Slow, fast-flying, pierces. Deadly at range.

The exact numbers
  • Sniper: half damage up close, full at six tiles, double at eight, triple from ten.
  • Shotgun: each pellet as strong as a Pistol shot, triple damage when all three land.
  • Railgun: two and a half times the Sniper at the same distance, triple past ten tiles.
  • Swing Blade: five spins in a row, then rests a second.
  • Chainsaw: 0.6x Strength per bite; four seconds without a break halves its speed until it rests a second.
