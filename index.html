Deep Descent

A roguelite dungeon shooter. Clear the rooms, spend your coins, beat the boss at the bottom of each chapter, and keep going down.

Chapter 2, Forest

Two hands, five weapons

You start with the Pistol and can carry two weapons. Shops sometimes sell another; trade the one in your hand and you can swap back for free before you leave. Every Damage upgrade makes all of them hit harder.

  • Pistol

    Steady and mid range. The one you start with.

  • Sniper Rifle

    Long range and slow. Half damage up close, double from twelve tiles away, and each shot bounces off one wall.

  • Shotgun

    Three pellets in a tight cone. Half damage each, so one and a half times the Pistol when they all land, but only at short range.

  • Railgun

    Slow to fire and fast to fly. Two and a half times the Pistol, and it hits harder still the farther the shot travels. Sold from the Forge.

  • Swing Blade

    A blade, not a gun: every two and a half seconds it spins once round you, hitting everything close and knocking enemy shots back the way they came.

Chapter 3, Glacier

How a floor goes

  1. Clear the rooms. The doors lock until every enemy is down. Slimes, archers, dashers, bouncers, embers and laser snipers come in groups that play off each other.
  2. Pick up the coins. They are your score, and your money.
  3. Shop and open chests. Upgrades stack into a build: Frost Rounds, Sniper Piercing, Magnet, Vampirism, Dash, Second Wind and more. Curses like Greed and Brittle pay well and cost you something.
  4. Take the portal down. Every fifth floor ends with a boss that has its own move to learn.

Five chapters of five floors, then an endless Arcade. Every floor comes from a seed, so a save code from the pause menu brings you back to the same floor in any browser.

Easy gives you more hearts. Hard changes how enemies fight: slimes split, archers lead their shots, dashers dash three times in a row. Beat the game once and Boss Rush opens: all five bosses back to back, against the clock, with two free picks between them.
